What to expect

Your first Sunday

Someone will meet you at the door, help you find a seat, and point you toward the coffee. There is no dress code — you will see everything from jeans to jackets, and nobody is counting.

A service usually includes singing together, prayer, a reading from the Bible, and a sermon of about thirty minutes. You are welcome to simply listen. We never single out guests or ask visitors to stand.

Afterward, most people stay for coffee. If you would like to meet a pastor, just ask anyone at the welcome table and they will introduce you.
