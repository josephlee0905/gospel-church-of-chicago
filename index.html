Who we are

A church gathered around Jesus, in the heart of Des Plaines

Gospel Church of Chicago is a congregation of families, students, and neighbors who gather each Sunday to worship God, study the Scriptures, and care for one another.

We began as a small group meeting in a living room, and we have never stopped being a place where people know each other by name. You will find honest preaching, singing together, and coffee afterward that goes on longer than it should.
