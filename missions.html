Where we serve

Latin America, near to us

Most of our mission work is in Latin America. Rev. Duk Lee travels there himself, several times a year, and has done so for many years. He was preaching in Brazil this September.

The work is mostly teaching. Pastors and church leaders gather for training seminars that run for several days — around tables, with Bibles and notebooks open — and then carry what they have learned back to their own congregations.

The rest of us support that work by praying for it and giving to it, and the church hears how it went when he returns.
