Who we are

A church family in Des Plaines

Gospel Church of Chicago is a congregation of families, students, and neighbors from across the northwest suburbs. Some of us have been here for decades; others arrived last month. We gather every Sunday to worship God together, to hear the Bible taught plainly, and to look after one another through the ordinary weeks of life.

We are a bilingual church. At ten o’clock each Sunday two services begin at once, in separate rooms — one in Korean and one in English — and afterwards everyone eats together. 한국어 안내를 보시려면 여기를 눌러 주세요.

What we believe

The convictions we hold in common

These are the simple, historic Christian beliefs that shape everything we do together.

The Scriptures
We believe the Bible is the word of God, and our final authority for what we believe and how we live.
God
We believe in one God, sovereign over all things, who made the world and still holds it in his care.
Jesus Christ
We believe Jesus is the Son of God, crucified in our place and raised from the dead, and that salvation is found in him alone.
Grace
We believe we are saved by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone, and not by anything we have earned.
The Church
We believe the church is Christ’s family, led by elders chosen from among the congregation, and sent to love one another and make Christ known.
Baptism and the Lord’s Supper
We believe these two signs were given to the church by Christ himself, as seals of God’s promise to his people.

Like Presbyterian churches before us, we hold to the Westminster Confession of Faith and the Shorter Catechism as faithful summaries of what the Bible teaches.

Our story

How we got here

Gospel Church of Chicago was founded in 1991 as Korean Jang-Sung Presbyterian Church. Three more names have followed since: Immanuel, then Oneness, and now Gospel.

Through every season, our purpose has stayed the same: to share the gospel to the ends of the earth.
