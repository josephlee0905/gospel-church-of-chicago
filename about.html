Who we are

A church family in Des Plaines

Gospel Church of Chicago is a congregation of families, students, and neighbours from across the northwest suburbs. Some of us have been here for decades; others arrived last month. We gather every Sunday to worship God together, to hear the Bible taught plainly, and to look after one another through the ordinary weeks of life.

We are a bilingual church community, and our services and ministries are held primarily in English, with a Korean-speaking community at the heart of our history. 한국어 안내를 보시려면 여기를 눌러 주세요.

What we believe

The convictions we hold in common

These are the simple, historic Christian beliefs that shape everything we do together.

The Scriptures
We believe the Bible is God’s word, trustworthy and sufficient for faith and for life.
Jesus Christ
We believe Jesus is the Son of God, crucified and risen, and that salvation is found in him alone.
Grace
We believe we are saved by grace through faith, not by anything we have earned or achieved.
The Church
We believe the church is a family sent to love one another and to make Christ known to our neighbours.
