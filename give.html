Give online

With Zelle, from your bank

Zelle is built into most banks’ apps, and it is the simplest way to give from home. Open your bank’s app, choose Zelle, and scan the code. If you are reading this on your phone, tap the code and choose your bank.

Before you send, check that the name reads Oneness Church of Chicago — the church’s legal name. The gift goes straight from your bank to the church’s. This website never sees it.

Questions

Good to know

Where does my gift go?
Gifts support Sunday worship, ministries for every age, the upkeep of our building, and our mission partners at home and abroad.
Is my gift tax-deductible?
Yes. The church is a registered 501(c)(3), and giving statements are issued at the end of the year.
Can I give to something specific?
Yes. A gift can be designated to missions, or to another particular need. Note it with your gift, or email us and we will see it goes where you intend.
Who can I ask?
